About the Project

How CineBook thinks

CineBook is a college AI mini-project. It is a genuine rule-based expert system — not machine learning.

1

Knowledge Base

JSON datasets of movies and books with their attributes.

2

IF–THEN Rules

Expert rules such as "IF mood is happy THEN prefer comedy".

3

Inference Engine

Checks your answers against the rules and scores each item by priority.

4

Explanation

Every recommendation shows exactly which rules fired.
